**The one number that was wrong, and it changes the routing:** PeerJ Computer Science is **not** offering a full APC waiver. That promotion ran in 2018 and ended April 2019. ## How to spend the one Access slot

IEEE Access is the only venue on this list that is both **fast** and **indexed**, and it costs real money.

Use it for the first of **chaos** or **migration** that has a finished experiment and a clean manuscript. Not for the error-budget magazine piece (that one is free). Not for observability (JNSM is free and now quotes a 31-day median first decision). Not as a second copy of the ACMSE burn-rate paper.

If the EB-1A lawyer prefers $0 subscription journals only, skip Access entirely. JNSM and TNSM still get you into SCIE without a fee.
